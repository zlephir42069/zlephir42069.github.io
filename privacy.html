Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Zlephiel Publisher is a private tool used only by Zlephiel Entertainment, the owner of the Zlephiel channels and accounts, to upload and manage its own content through the YouTube Data API, the TikTok Content Posting API and Login Kit, and the Instagram API. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

Only the owner's own account data needed to upload videos and manage them: basic profile information (such as account name and ID), and the titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, schedules, playlists and captions of the owner's own content. No data from any other user is collected.

Use and sharing

The data is used only to manage the owner's own accounts. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's own machine. Access can be revoked at any time in Google account permissions (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), TikTok's Manage app permissions settings, or Meta's Business Integrations settings. Revoking access and deleting the stored tokens removes all data held by the tool.

Third-party terms

This tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Use of TikTok and Instagram is also subject to their own terms and privacy policies.
